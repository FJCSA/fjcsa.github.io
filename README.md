# FRONTIERS FOR JUST CITIES IN SOUTH ASIA
A network for and by Early Career Researchers(ECRs) from South Asia, working across the region on issues of urban governance in the Global South.
Bringing South Asian unique perspectives and positionalities to reclaim space in global urban discourses
Open to PhD researchers, early-career and mid-career scholars

Who we are
We are a research network led by early career scholars working on South Asian cities. Our network is committed to foregrounding positive and impact-led South Asian cases and theories to inform global discourses. This is a unique platform for PhD researchers, and early- and mid-career scholars to share ground-breaking and innovative research, foster dialogues and knowledge exchanges, and explore cross-regional collaborations across and beyond South Asia.
